About

I’m Yidong He, a designer and curious builder working across AI products and interaction design. I’m passionate about turning my “what ifs” into immersive, creative, and thoughtful experiences. At ByteDance and 51WORLD, I’ve worked on AI product strategy, agent evaluation, and interaction design, connecting people’s needs with what AI can do.

I’m currently pursuing a master’s at Harvard Graduate School of Design, where I co-chair Harvard Urban AI. Before that, I studied architecture at Rice University—a background that still shapes my attention to how things look, feel, and work.
