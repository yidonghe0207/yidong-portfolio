How can an AI agent turn a venue team’s request into the right action?

Venue operations and sales teams need to work with schedules, booking information and physical spaces. AIC brings an AI agent and interactive 3D tools together in an on-premises event-planning platform.

At 51WORLD, I translated user needs into product requirements and interactions, refined training data to improve agent reliability, and supported client validation and delivery.

01 / Defining the Workflow

Make a business request specific enough to build.

I translated user needs into 10+ core intents for the event-planning agent. For the Shenzhen International Exchange Center, I designed calendar, booking-query and venue overview/detail interactions.

Turn the proposal into a shared reference.

I used prototypes, documentation and working sessions to communicate proposals to developers, making the intended interactions concrete enough to discuss and implement.

02 / Improving Reliability

The agent needed to choose and use the right tool.

Incorrect tool selection and parameter errors could prevent a request from becoming the intended action. I developed and refined training data for the locally deployed model to address both.

60%→85%+

Combined intent-recognition and tool-call accuracy

I helped improve this measure through dataset optimization, evaluated against a recurring test set.
