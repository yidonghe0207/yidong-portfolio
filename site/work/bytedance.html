How do we know an AI video agent is ready for real creative work?

A striking video is only part of the answer. A useful agent also needs to understand a brief, preserve creative intent across revisions, and deliver a coherent result.

At ByteDance, I worked on product strategy and evaluation for Seedance-powered video creation agents across Doubao and the Ark API. I translated creator workflows and product gaps into testable requirements, shared quality standards, and recommendations for product and algorithm teams.

    Judge impact, recurrence and cause.

01 / Finding Product Gaps

Start with what creators need to accomplish.

I studied competing agents and public creator workflows, including advertising films, then compared those needs with our agent’s capabilities.

Turn workflows into requirements.

I built a 100-task evaluation set covering instruction following, consistency and revisions, then a 40-task short-drama set to examine narrative workflows more closely.

Separate the symptom from the cause.

I reviewed finished videos first, then the agent’s recorded steps. This distinguished a poor creative result from the planning, tool or generation issue behind it.

Finding from my reviews

Creators needed control the tools did not offer.

I identified missing audio and visual-style anchors in the generation tools. This raised a concrete product question: what controls would help creators preserve their intent across a sequence?

02 / Setting Priorities

Identify which problems deserve attention first.

I combined output usability, recurring failure patterns and scenario-level comparisons to support priority discussions. Diagnostic labels and execution traces helped connect each problem to a specific area for improvement.

Example: why the failure stage changes the next step +

If a presenter changes after an edit, a lost constraint points to planning; a correctly passed constraint with a mismatched output points to generation. I used this kind of trace analysis to narrow the investigation before recommending a direction.

03 / Recommending Changes

Turn a finding into a focused next step.

I brought representative cases and trace findings to product and algorithm teams, connecting the creator-facing problem to a specific area for improvement.

Subtitle failures → generation feedback

I flagged subtitle problems in generated videos to product and engineering. Locating the issue at the generation layer gave the team a specific capability to investigate.

Version differences → iteration decisions

I analyzed prompt and orchestration changes through comparisons of agent versions, models and competitors. Reports highlighted recurring weaknesses and scenario-level differences to inform optimization and model selection.

I carried this work into pre-release reviews for Doubao, preparing agent-versus-model comparisons to support the team’s release discussions.
